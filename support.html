Vane: Desktop Weather Support

Vane is a weather app for your Mac: current conditions, a 7-day forecast with hourly detail, a wind vane, an animated radar map and a desktop widget.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the city?

Type a city name in the search field at the top of the window and pick a result. You can add a region to narrow it down, for example "Moab, UT". Vane remembers your last choice.

How do I switch between metric and imperial units?

Use the units control next to the search field. The change applies immediately and also updates the widget.

How do I add the widget?

Right-click the desktop or open Notification Center, choose Edit Widgets, and search for Vane.

The forecast or radar isn't loading.

Vane needs an internet connection. Click the refresh button to try again. Forecast data comes from Open-Meteo and radar from RainViewer, so a temporary outage at either service can cause gaps.

Does Vane use my location?

No. You choose a city by searching for it, and Vane never asks for your device location.
