Vane: Desktop Weather Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Vane: Desktop Weather ("the app") is a weather app for Mac, published by Jaye Mondale. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The developer has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and cannot see which places you look up.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Your selected place and your unit preference are saved on your Mac so the app and its widget can show your last forecast. They are not sent to the developer.

Location

The app does not access your device's location. You choose a city by searching for it.

Third-party services

To work, the app requests data from services run by other companies. Like any internet request, these requests reveal your IP address to the service.

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) receives the city name you search for, and the coordinates of the place you select, to return place matches and forecasts.
  • RainViewer (rainviewer.com) receives requests for radar map tiles covering the area shown on the radar map.
  • Apple Maps provides the base map under the radar, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

The developer does not receive any of this data. Each service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
